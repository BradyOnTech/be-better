# Be Better

One athlete follows a training program, plans sessions, and records what actually happened.

## Language

**Program pattern**:
A reference describing the purpose, progression, recovery, and representative workouts of a kind of training.
_Avoid_: Active program, calendar

**Training program**:
The athlete's chosen training direction over a dated block, with a weekly commitment and a schedule they explicitly choose to follow.
_Avoid_: Workout library, program pattern

**Planned session**:
A dated prescription describing what the athlete intends to do and why.
_Avoid_: Completed activity

**Activity**:
A confirmed record of training the athlete actually completed, regardless of what was planned.
_Avoid_: Planned session

**Session outcome**:
The athlete's explicit account of how an activity relates to a planned session: completed as planned, modified, or replaced. A missed session is skipped; additional training leaves the planned session outstanding.
_Avoid_: Automatic match

**Plan review**:
A pending choice about whether a logged activity fulfilled, modified, replaced, or was additional to a planned session.

**Decision note**:
A dated explanation preserving the reason for a change to an accepted plan or a session outcome.
